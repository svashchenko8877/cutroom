Cutroom – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Data we collect

Cutroom does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. The app has no accounts, no servers, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking.

Data stored on your iPhone

Your projects, imported media copies and exports are stored only inside the app on your iPhone. Exported videos are saved to your photo library only when you choose to export. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Permissions

Photo library access is used only to let you pick videos and photos to edit and to save exported videos. The microphone is used only while you record a voiceover. Speech recognition is used only if you tap Captions, and runs on your device whenever your device supports it (otherwise Apple's speech service may be used, as described in Apple's privacy policy). Cutroom never receives this audio or text.

Children

Cutroom collects no information from anyone, including children.
