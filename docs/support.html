Cutroom – Support

Report a problem or ask a question

Open an issue at github.com/svashchenko8877/cutroom/issues and include your iPhone model and iOS version. Please do not post private videos.

My export is slow

4K exports with filters take longer. Try 1080p, keep Cutroom open and your iPhone unlocked while it renders.

Captions or voiceover do not work

Open Settings > Cutroom and allow Microphone and Speech Recognition.

Where are my videos?

Exports are saved to your Photos library. Projects stay inside the app until you delete them.
